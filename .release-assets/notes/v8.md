# v8 — Manaweaver Patch 8 / 魔能术士 Patch 8

## 中文
当前推荐版本。修复 v7 `.loca` 文本偏移导致的乱码问题，并加入三项平衡限制：

- 魔能精通：因魔能爆 Miss 获得附赠动作，每回合最多触发1次；
- 噬法魔爆：击杀返还邪术师契约法术位，每回合最多1次；
- 魔能之心III：每场战斗最多复活1次，每次长休之间最多复活3次。

保留魔能术士专属成长、9项魔能被动、魔能之心体系、专属法表，以及魔能爆裂 / 魔爆倾泻 / 魔能湮灭等核心内容。

## English
Current recommended release. Fixes the v7 `.loca` text-offset corruption and adds three balance limits:

- Mana Mastery: Bonus Action from a missed Eldritch Blast can trigger at most once per round;
- Devouring Blast: pact-slot restoration from kills can trigger at most once per round;
- Mana Heart III: at most one revival per combat and three revivals between Long Rests.

Retains the Manaweaver-exclusive progression, nine Mana passives, Mana Heart system, custom spell list, and the core custom spells Mana Detonation / Eldritch Barrage / Mana Annihilation.
